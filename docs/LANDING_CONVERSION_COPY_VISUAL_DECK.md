# Delirio Landing Conversion Copy and Visual Deck

**Status:** Proposed for founder review. Not yet implemented.  
**Primary objective:** Qualified product conversion.  
**Primary audience:** GLP-1 users, led by time-constrained professional women who want sustainable strength and whose consistency does not sustain itself when life changes.  
**Canonical strategy:** [`GLP1_CONTENT_MOMENTUM_STRATEGY.md`](./GLP1_CONTENT_MOMENTUM_STRATEGY.md)

## 1. Recommended page order

1. Header (the separate availability strip was removed on 2026-08-04 — see §3.1)
2. Hero: recognition, desired outcome, and product action
3. Problem and reframe: present friction, consequence, and removal of self-blame
4. Plan-to-live product journey: differentiated mechanism
5. Voice coaching demonstration: immediate proof
6. Coach selection and live studio: interactive proof and agency
7. AI trust layer: capability, limits, privacy, and control
8. Pricing: value and financial feasibility
9. FAQ: remaining objections
10. Secondary research invitation: Shape What's Next
11. Footer: final product action

This order differs from the current page. The plan-to-live journey should move before the voice demonstration and coach studio because it explains the complete mechanism before asking the visitor to interact with one part of it.

## 2. Conversion hierarchy

### Recommended primary action

Use the official App Store badge or `DOWNLOAD DELIRIO` as the primary commercial action.

### Recommended secondary action

Use `MEET YOUR AI COACH` or `TRY VOICE COACHING` as the product-experience action.

### Recommended research action

Keep `SHAPE WHAT'S NEXT` as a secondary research invitation after product proof. Label it clearly as a short questionnaire that helps shape the product. Do not use `TAKE A QUIZ`, because the questionnaire does not produce a personalized result.

If customer discovery is temporarily more important than App Store adoption, the hierarchy may be reversed as an explicit experiment. Do not let the visual design imply that both are equally primary.

### 3.12 FAQ architecture

Reduce the current FAQ and promote GLP-1 questions into the first category.

#### Category 1: GLP-1 and strength

**Can Delirio support strength training while I use a GLP-1-based medication?**

> Delirio can adjust fitness planning around the time, energy, comfort, schedule, and readiness you choose to share. It does not prescribe medication, diagnose symptoms, provide medical or nutrition treatment, or replace your healthcare professional.

**What if my readiness changes from one day to another?**

> Tell your coach what feels realistic today. Delirio can offer a shorter, simpler, moved, or rested fitness option while keeping the larger plan connected. It cannot determine whether medication caused the change.

**Can Delirio prevent muscle loss?**

> No. Delirio does not guarantee muscle preservation or any medical outcome. It provides fitness planning and coaching designed to help you create and continue a realistic strength routine.

**What if I am maintaining, tapering, stopping, or losing access to medication?**

> Delirio can keep the fitness plan focused on the goals, schedule, readiness, and training feedback you provide. Medication changes belong with your prescribing healthcare professional.

**What if I feel sick, dizzy, unusually weak, or otherwise unwell?**

> Pause the workout. Delirio cannot assess the symptom or determine whether it is safe for you to exercise. Follow your clinician's guidance and your medication's official instructions, and contact an appropriate healthcare professional when symptoms are severe, persistent, unusual, or concerning.

#### Category 2: AI and privacy

**Are Iris and Reed AI?**

> Yes. Iris and Reed are AI fitness coaches. They generate responses from the conversation and supported workout context available to Delirio. They can misunderstand or be wrong, and they are not human trainers or medical professionals.

**What can the camera assess?**

> When you choose to use it, Delirio uses pose estimation for supported movements and visible movement details. Camera angle, lighting, clothing, and body visibility can limit the result. It does not diagnose injury or guarantee correct form.

**Do I have to use the camera or microphone?**

> No. Camera and microphone access are optional. You can follow the plan and use text without them. Some live movement or voice features will not be available when access is off.

**What information does Delirio retain?**

> Delirio retains information according to your settings and the Privacy Policy so supported features can work. Review the policy for current details about conversations, workout data, camera processing, providers, retention, and deletion controls.

#### Category 3: Coaching and product

**How is Delirio different from a typical fitness app?**

> A typical app may give you a plan, videos, and timers as separate tools. Delirio connects planning, the workout, questions, reflection, and return through one continuing AI coach.

**Does Delirio replace a personal trainer?**

> No. Delirio can plan, explain, adapt, and help carry context between sessions. It cannot physically spot you and does not provide the judgment, credentials, or hands-on assistance of a qualified human professional.

**What is the difference between Iris and Reed?**

> Iris is warm and attentive. Reed is direct and structured. Both are AI fitness coaches using the supported Delirio context available to them. You can switch later.

**What happens if I miss time and return?**

> Delirio can use the context retained under your settings to help prepare the next fitness step without asking you to reconstruct everything from memory.

**Can I use Delirio at home, while traveling, or in a gym?**

> Yes. Your coach can plan around the space, time, and equipment you report. Camera-based features require a suitable phone position and enough visible space, but camera use is optional.

#### Category 4: Price and subscription

**What is included?**

> Monthly and annual subscriptions include the same supported Delirio coaching experience: adaptive planning, voice and text, workout guidance, and continuity between sessions.

**Why does Delirio cost $30 per month?**

> The monthly plan provides the full supported coaching experience without an annual commitment. The annual plan is $180 per year, equivalent to $15 per month.

**How do I manage or cancel the subscription?**

> App Store subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Review the subscription details shown by Apple before purchase and use your App Store subscription settings to manage renewal or cancellation.

Do not retain `No contracts, no cancellation fees` until the exact Apple subscription behavior and applicable terms have been verified.

## 4. Photo and graphic recommendations by page surface

### 4.1 Global visual narrative

Use one recurring lead protagonist across the hero and product-journey photography. This creates story continuity and improves recognition more than unrelated stock photos.

Lead casting direction:

- Woman approximately 40 to 55
- Professional but not luxury-coded
- Midsize or ordinary changing body
- Credible beginner-to-intermediate strength experience
- Capable and self-directed, not helpless
- Comfortable appearing at home, at work, and in an ordinary gym

Secondary representation should include men, broader ages, multiple body sizes, racial and ethnic diversity, different training histories, and different environments.

Avoid visual cues that imply GLP-1 use can be identified from appearance.

### 4.2 Hero photo

**Recommended scene**

A professional woman transitions from a real workday into strength training in an apartment, home gym, or calm neighborhood gym. The frame may include a laptop closing, phone nearby, and ordinary dumbbells or a bench. She should look focused and capable, not exhausted or elite.

**Composition**

- Wide landscape crop with quiet negative space for the heading
- Subject on the right third when copy is on the left
- Natural directional light
- Real movement or preparation rather than a static fitness pose
- Phone visible only if natural
- No injection, medication package, scale, measuring tape, or transformation cue

**Why it converts**

It answers “Does this see my life?” before asking the visitor to care about product mechanics.

### 4.3 Problem and reframe band

**Preferred treatment**

Use typography and three small lived-context details rather than three generic fitness photos:

1. A calendar block being moved after a work meeting changes.
2. A paused set with the user looking at the plan rather than posing in distress.
3. A phone reopening the same training thread after several missed days.

These may be cropped editorial photographs or restrained interface-plus-life composites.

**Why it converts**

The visuals make the friction concrete without using shame or dramatic body imagery.

### 4.4 Plan-to-live journey photography

Keep real product screenshots in the shared matte iPhone frame. Use cinematic photos only as supporting context.

#### Plan

The lead protagonist reviews the day while finishing work or sitting at a kitchen table. Show the phone as a planning tool, not a social-media distraction.

#### Start

The same person prepares an ordinary strength session by setting down a work bag, tying shoes, or adjusting a bench. Avoid athletic starting-line imagery.

#### Ask

Show a full-body strength movement with an achievable load and a naturally positioned phone. The frame must communicate a real question moment without implying injury.

#### Review

Show the same person between or after sets, calmly reviewing the phone. Avoid collapse, pain, or exaggerated exhaustion.

#### Return

Show the same person reopening the training thread after a workday, trip, or missed period. The emotional tone is relief and continuity, not guilt.

**Production rule**

The five images should look like one story. Match subject, wardrobe family, lighting language, and environment. Do not assemble five unrelated stock athletes.

### 4.5 Voice coaching demonstration graphic

Do not add another photograph. Use the actual phone screenshot, waveform, and a simple three-step conversation graphic:

1. `YOU REPORTED`
2. `COACH EXPLAINED`
3. `YOU CHOSE`

This demonstrates agency and mechanism more directly than lifestyle photography.

### 4.6 Coach studio graphic

Use the real interactive UI as the primary visual. Keep Iris and Reed equally discoverable. Their portraits may remain stylized, but add a visible `AI FITNESS COACH` label near selection and session entry.

Avoid clinical badges, human credentials, glowing “intelligence” effects, or realistic human portraiture that could imply a live person.

### 4.7 AI trust graphic

Use an inspectable information-flow diagram rather than a futuristic diamond, diagnostic score, or medical-study graphic.

Recommended structure:

```text
YOU PROVIDE                DELIRIO CAN SUPPORT             DELIRIO DOES NOT DECIDE
Goal                       Plan and explain               Diagnosis
Time                       Shorten or move                Medication
Equipment                  Guide supported workouts      Medical clearance
Readiness                  Carry context forward         Symptom causality
```

Add visible controls beneath it:

`REVIEW / CORRECT / PAUSE / RETRY / SWITCH / STOP`

### 4.8 Pricing graphics

Use no photography. Pricing converts through clarity. Keep both plans aligned, show the exact billing period, make the annual comparison mathematically transparent, and use one official App Store action per card or one shared action after selection.

Avoid “best value” unless the comparison basis is visible. Prefer `LOWEST MONTHLY EQUIVALENT`.

### 4.9 Questionnaire graphic

Use no lifestyle photo inside the modal. The participant should focus on one question and no more than four answers. The full-width progress bar, stable Close, Back, and immediate selection state are sufficient.

If a pre-launch invitation surface needs imagery, use a restrained crop of the same lead protagonist using text privately. Do not use medical charts or injection imagery.

### 4.10 FAQ graphic

Use no decorative photo. The FAQ is a high-intent reading and trust surface. Typography, category organization, readable IDs, clear answers, and nearby contact support should carry it.

### 4.11 Footer graphic

Use the Delirio logo, Delirio wordmark, and official App Store badge. No additional photo is needed.

## 5. Image sourcing recommendation

Preferred order:

1. A one-day custom shoot with one lead protagonist and a planned five-scene narrative.

The conversion value comes from narrative consistency and audience recognition, not merely image resolution.
