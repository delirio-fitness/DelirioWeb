**Last updated:** 2026-09-04

## How App Store search works

Apple's algorithm uses three metadata fields to determine where to rank your app:

1. **App Name** (30 characters max) — Highest keyword weight
2. **Subtitle** (30 characters max) — Second highest weight
3. **Keyword Field** (100 characters, hidden, comma-separated) — Additional keywords

Keywords earlier in the name get more weight. Keywords are never repeated across fields — Apple combines them automatically and penalizes duplication.

### Keyword field strategy

The keyword field is 100 characters, comma-separated, invisible to users. Rules:

- **No spaces after commas** — wastes characters
- **No duplicates** from name or subtitle — Apple combines them, duplication wastes space and hurts rank
- **Singular forms only** — Apple matches both "workout" and "workouts" from "workout"
- **No competitor brand names** — Apple rejects updates or removes apps for this
- **Include misspellings only if common** — generally not worth the character cost

## ASO conversion optimization (beyond keywords)

Keywords get people to your listing. These convert them to downloads:

1. **Screenshots** — First 3 visible without scrolling. Show: coach conversation, adaptive plan, live session guidance
2. **App Preview Video** — 15-30 second demo of the coaching experience. Apple gives video listings higher conversion
3. **Ratings & Reviews** — Ask satisfied users to rate. Apps with 4.5+ stars convert 2-3x better
4. **Description first paragraph** — Most users don't scroll. Lead with the value proposition, not features
