# Website SEO Keywords — Delirio

**Last updated:** 2026-09-04

## Keyword strategy overview

Delirio is a new domain with no search authority. Head terms like "fitness app" or "personal trainer app" are dominated by established players (Fitbod, Future, Nike Training Club) and media sites (CNET, Tom's Guide, Forbes). Competing there directly is not realistic in the short term.

**The strategy:** Target long-tail, high-intent keywords where:
1. The searcher has a specific problem Delirio solves
2. Current results are thin, generic, or dominated by listicle roundups
3. The search intent aligns with someone who would actually download the app

---

## Tier 1 — GLP-1 + Fitness (Delirio's unique positioning)

These keywords represent the biggest opportunity. Search volume for GLP-1 + exercise topics has exploded since 2023 and continues to grow. PubMed shows research publications jumping from 1 paper in 2021 to 13 in 2026 alone. Current results are dominated by medical sites giving generic advice — almost no fitness apps are creating content here.

| Keyword cluster | Example queries | Est. competition | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ozempic + strength training** | "strength training on ozempic", "ozempic workout plan", "best exercises while on ozempic" | Low-Medium | Informational → Product |
| **GLP-1 + muscle loss** | "how to prevent muscle loss on ozempic", "losing muscle on wegovy", "glp-1 muscle preservation" | Medium | Informational → Product |
| **Wegovy/Mounjaro + exercise** | "exercise plan while on wegovy", "mounjaro workout routine", "zepbound and exercise" | Low | Informational → Product |
| **GLP-1 + adaptive fitness** | "fitness app for ozempic users", "workout app for glp-1" | Very Low | Transactional |
| **GLP-1 + energy changes** | "low energy workout on ozempic", "ozempic fatigue exercise" | Very Low | Informational → Product |
| **GLP-1 + returning to fitness** | "starting to exercise on ozempic", "rebuilding strength after weight loss" | Low | Informational → Product |

### Why this is the biggest opportunity

- **Volume is growing rapidly.** GLP-1 prescriptions expected to reach 30M+ in the US by 2027.
- **Current results are thin.** Medical sites give generic advice. No fitness app is targeting this audience.
- **The audience matches Delirio's product beliefs.** Your product constitution explicitly targets GLP-1 users.
- **High intent.** Someone searching "strength training plan for ozempic" is actively looking for a solution.

## Tier 3 — Problem-Intent Keywords

Target people experiencing problems Delirio solves, before they know the solution exists.

| Keyword cluster | Example queries | Est. competition | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Workout motivation** | "lost motivation to work out", "can't stick to workout plan" | High | Informational |
| **Returning to fitness** | "getting back into working out after a long break" | Medium | Informational |
| **Workout planning fatigue** | "tired of planning my own workouts", "don't know what to do at the gym" | Medium | Info → Product |
| **Adaptive training** | "workout plan that adjusts to me", "flexible workout program" | Low-Medium | Transactional |
| **Home workout coaching** | "home workout with coaching", "guided home workout app" | Medium | Transactional |

## Tier 4 — Branded & Navigational

Low volume now, increases with marketing. Must rank #1 for all of these.

- "delirio app" / "delirio fitness" — Brand search
- "delirio ai coach" — Brand + feature
- "iris delirio coach" / "reed delirio coach" — Coach-specific

---

## Keywords to AVOID (for now)

Dominated by massive sites requiring years of authority:

- "best fitness app" — CNET, Forbes, Tom's Guide
- "workout app" / "personal trainer app" — established players
- "weight loss app" — medical sites, regulatory sensitivity
