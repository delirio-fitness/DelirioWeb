# Research Sources & How to Get Real Keyword Data

**Last updated:** 2026-09-04

## The honest truth about keyword volume estimates

The keyword strategy documents in this folder are based on competitive analysis, published market data, and search landscape observation — **not** on actual search volume numbers from keyword research tools. Real search volume data requires paid tool access.

This document lists the credible sources used, what data they provided, and exactly how to get the actual keyword volume numbers yourself.

---

## Confirmed data from credible sources

### GLP-1 market size (KFF Health Tracking Poll, May 2024)
**Source:** https://www.kff.org/health-costs/poll-finding/kff-health-tracking-poll-may-2024-the-publics-use-and-views-of-glp-1-drugs/

- **12% of US adults** (roughly 30+ million people) say they have ever taken a GLP-1 drug
- **6% of US adults** say they are currently taking one
- **43% of adults with diabetes** have taken a GLP-1
- **22% of adults told they are overweight/obese** have taken a GLP-1
- This was May 2024 data — adoption has continued growing since

**Why this matters for SEO:** Every current and former GLP-1 user is a potential searcher for exercise guidance. 6% of US adults = ~15 million current users, all of whom eventually ask "how should I exercise on this medication?"

### Health & Fitness app revenue benchmarks (RevenueCat, 2025)
**Source:** https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/

- Health & Fitness apps: **$0.63 revenue per install** at 60 days (tied with AI apps for highest)
- Overall median across all categories: $0.31 revenue per install
- Health & Fitness has the **lowest refund rate** (1.51%) of any category
- ~30% of annual subscriptions canceled in first month across all categories
- Low-priced annual plans retain up to **36%** of subscribers after a year

### Published research on GLP-1 + exercise (PubMed, accessed September 2026)
**Source:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=GLP-1+exercise+strength+training+muscle+mass

- **34 total papers** on GLP-1 + exercise + strength training + muscle mass
- Publication timeline: 1 (2021), 2 (2023), 7 (2024), 11 (2025), 13 (2026)
- Research is accelerating rapidly — confirming growing scientific and public interest
- Key finding across studies: resistance training preserves lean mass during GLP-1 weight loss

### Top Google searches (Ahrefs, September 2026)
**Source:** https://ahrefs.com/blog/top-google-searches/

- Ahrefs tracks 28.7 billion keywords
- No fitness-specific terms appear in the top 100 US searches
- This confirms fitness keywords are long-tail/niche — not head terms
- Ahrefs is the tool to use for actual volume data on specific keywords

## How to get real keyword volume numbers

### Option 1: Google Keyword Planner (Free with Google Ads account)
**Source:** https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243

- **What it gives you:** Monthly search volume ranges, competition level, cost-per-click estimates
- **How to access:** Create a free Google Ads account → Tools → Keyword Planner
- **Limitation:** Shows volume *ranges* (e.g., "1K-10K") unless you're spending on ads, then you get exact numbers
- **Important caveat from Google:** "Personalized advertising and data privacy policies often prevent results for keywords in sensitive categories like health" — some GLP-1 and health keywords may show limited data
- **Keywords to research:** Enter each keyword cluster from `website-seo-keywords.md` and record actual volumes

### Option 2: Ahrefs Keywords Explorer ($99/month — most accurate for SEO)
- **What it gives you:** Exact monthly search volume, keyword difficulty score (0-100), click-through rate, parent topic, SERP analysis
- **Best for:** Understanding which keywords you can realistically rank for based on your domain authority
- **Free alternative:** Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (free, but limited to keywords your site already ranks for)

### Option 3: Google Search Console (Free — you already have this)
- **What it gives you:** Actual queries people used to find your site, impressions, clicks, average position
- **Limitation:** Only shows data for queries where your site already appeared in results
- **Best for:** Discovering keywords you're accidentally ranking for and optimizing those

### Option 4: Google Trends (Free)
**Source:** https://trends.google.com/trends/
- **What it gives you:** Relative interest over time (not absolute volume), related queries, geographic breakdown
- **Best for:** Comparing keyword popularity (e.g., "ozempic workout" vs "wegovy exercise"), confirming trends are growing
- **Keywords to compare:** Enter GLP-1 + exercise combinations to see which drug names have most search interest
