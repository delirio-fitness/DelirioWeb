# Content Strategy — Delirio

**Last updated:** 2026-09-04

## Why content matters now

Delirio has 2 indexable pages. Every competitor with a blog has 20-100+ pages indexed. Each page is another entry point from Google. The technical SEO is solid — the missing piece is content that matches what people actually search for.

## Content format recommendations

1. **Lead with the question.** Title = the exact thing someone would search.
2. **Answer immediately.** Don't bury the answer under 500 words of preamble.
3. **Include Delirio naturally.** Show how the app handles this specific problem — but the article must be genuinely useful even without the app.
4. **Medical disclaimer on all GLP-1 content.** Required — align with claims-ledger.md boundaries.
5. **1,500-2,500 words per guide.** Long enough to rank, short enough to be readable.
6. **One target keyword cluster per page.** Don't dilute by targeting everything on one page.
